Privacy Policy

Nyang Nyang Bus Escape (냥냥 버스 탈출) · Android package com.pureflon.nyangbus · Last updated: 2026-11-28 · Effective: 2026-11-28

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Nyang Nyang Bus Escape, a mobile game published by hesse (Seoul, South Korea) on Google Play. It explains what information is collected when you install or play the game, how it is used, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or postal address. The following is collected automatically while you play:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, country/region, time zone, app version.
  • Identifiers: an app installation / device identifier used to count players and sessions, and the Android advertising ID used to show and measure ads.
  • App activity: session start and length, and in-game events such as level started, level completed, score and retries, and interactions with ads (ad shown, tapped, watched).
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data such as app launch time.
  • IP address, used to determine approximate location (country/region), to show ads and to prevent abuse. It is not used to identify you.

3. How we use information

  • To operate the game and fix problems.
  • To measure how many people play, how long they play and where they stop, so we can improve the game.
  • To measure the results of our own marketing (for example, how many installs an ad campaign brought).
  • To show ads in the game and measure how they perform.

We do not sell your information. The game shows ads and has no in-app purchases.

4. Services that process data for us

  • GameAnalytics — gameplay analytics. Privacy policy
  • Supercent log service — gameplay and session logs, operated by Supercent, Inc. (Seoul, South Korea), the developer of the Maden platform used to build and publish this game.
  • Meta (Facebook SDK) — app event and install measurement for our marketing. Privacy policy
  • Google Play services — app distribution. Privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — in-game advertising. Google collects and shares your IP address (approximate location), the advertising ID and other device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics including crash logs, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. How Google uses data · Privacy policy

GameAnalytics, the Supercent log service and Meta process the data above only to provide their service to us. Google AdMob also uses the data it collects for its own advertising purposes, as described in Google's policy, and ads may be personalised based on your advertising ID.

5. Data security and retention

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Analytics and log data are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted or anonymised.
  • Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game.

6. Your choices and rights

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads). Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. To make a request, email hesse@supercent.io with the subject "Nyang Nyang Bus Escape data request". We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
